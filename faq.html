Travel CRM Questions

Travel CRM is our SaaS product built specifically for Indian travel agencies. It manages your entire business in one system — leads (7-stage pipeline), tour management with approval workflow, bookings lifecycle, payments with GST & Razorpay, auto-invoices (PDF), commission system, and reports. Multi-role access for Admin, Manager, Agent, Finance and Customer. Includes Itinerary Builder, Customer Portal with OTP/Gmail login, and WhatsApp notifications. See full product details →

Travel CRM is currently free during early access. We are onboarding agencies at no cost — full platform, no limits, no credit card. Pricing will come later, and early access agencies get the best rate locked in. See early access details.

Yes. We handle the full setup — system configuration, branding, user accounts and data migration. We also train you and your team before go-live. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use Travel CRM.

Yes. The Itinerary Builder is a core module. You can build day-by-day itineraries, attach pricing, generate a PDF proposal, and share a public URL with your customer. When the customer approves, the system automatically converts it to a booking. The full lifecycle is: DRAFT → SENT → VIEWED → APPROVED → CONVERTED.

Yes. The Customer Portal lets your clients log in via OTP or Gmail, browse your tours, book directly, and pay via Razorpay — all without calling your office. They can also view their booking history and download receipts.
