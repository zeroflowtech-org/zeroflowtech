Everything Your Agency Needs — Already Built

17 production-ready modules. Not a prototype — a complete SaaS deployed and running for agencies right now.

Lead Pipeline

7-stage funnel: New → Contacted → Qualified → Proposal Sent → Negotiation → Converted / Lost. Every enquiry tracked. Nothing slips.

Itinerary Builder

Day-by-day planner, pricing engine, proposal PDF. Customer receives a public proposal link — they approve online. One click converts to booking.

Tour Management

Build tour packages with pricing tiers. Approval workflow: Draft → Review → Live. Agents only see live, approved tours.

Booking Lifecycle

Full booking flow — Draft to Confirmed to Completed. Auto-calculates base price, markup, GST. Invoice PDF generated instantly on confirmation.

Payments + Razorpay

Online payments via Razorpay + offline cash/UPI/NEFT. Partial payments, full payments, GST tracking. Tally-compatible GST summary export.

Commission System

Commissions auto-created on booking. Agent → Manager approval flow: Pending → Approved → Paid. Agent gets WhatsApp + in-app notification at every step.

Reports + Export

GST summary, aging receivables, payment methods breakdown, agent performance leaderboard. Date-range filters, CSV export for Tally.

Customer Portal

Your customers browse tours, book online, pay via Razorpay — without calling you. OTP or Gmail OAuth login. Booking history, invoice downloads.

Notifications + Audit

Real-time in-app alerts for new leads, booking status, commission approvals. Full audit trail — every action logged with timestamps.
